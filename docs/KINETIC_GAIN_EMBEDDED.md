# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Board Decision Ownership Reset Brief is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- convert handoff sprawl, owner drift, and approval conflict into explicit board-visible ownership-reset tradeoffs
- expose where named final ownership or approver clarification can recover decision speed before another lane is funded
- tie ownership coverage and board confidence to named operating surfaces instead of abstract accountability complaints
- convert scattered ownership bottlenecks into one reusable intervention packet for boards and investors
